SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION (SRS)
Project: Research Consensus Council
Version: 1.0

1. INTRODUCTION
1.1 Purpose
The Research Consensus Council is an automated multi-agent AI system designed to review research papers, deliberate on their quality, and produce a consensus verdict (Accept, Minor Revisions, Major Revisions, or Reject) along with a detailed feedback report.

1.2 Scope
The system will accept Research Papers (PDF/Text) as input, extract necessary content (abstract, methods, results, claims), and activate a council of 5 specialized reviewer agents. These agents will engage in a multi-round debate, score the paper across distinct criteria, and output an aggregate decision.

2. OVERALL DESCRIPTION
2.1 System Architecture
- Input Module: Accepts PDF/Text research papers.
- Content Extractor: Extracts and structures Abstract, Methods, Results, and Claims.
- Agent Council: Activates 5 reviewer agents (Skeptical Reviewer, Method Evaluator, Domain Expert, Ethics Officer, Industry Translator).
- Deliberation Engine: Orchestrates debate, score generation, and limitation identification.
- Output Generator: Aggregates scores and compiles the detailed feedback report.

2.2 Scoring & Decision Logic
The final verdict is based on a weighted 1-5 scale:
- Clarity & Presentation (20%)
- Methodology Rigor (25%)
- Novelty & Significance (20%)
- Ethics & Integrity (20%)
- Practical Impact (15%)

Verdict Thresholds:
- 4.5 - 5.0: Accept
- 3.5 - 4.4: Minor Revisions
- 2.5 - 3.4: Major Revisions
- < 2.5: Reject

3. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
- FR1: The system shall parse PDF and Text files.
- FR2: The system shall instantiate 5 distinct LLM-based agents.
- FR3: The system shall conduct multi-round debates (Initial reviews, Responses to critiques, Final positions).
- FR4: The system shall calculate an aggregate score using the predefined weighting matrix.
- FR5: The system shall support Appeals and Rebuttals from authors.

4. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
- NFR1 (Transparency): Full review chain and agent reasoning must be logged and visible.
- NFR2 (Quality Control): System must support bias detection in scoring and allow for monthly accuracy audits.
