ARCHITECTURE DOCUMENT
System: Research Consensus Council

1. HIGH-LEVEL WORKFLOW
- Input Module: Accepts Research Paper (PDF/Text).
- Content Extractor: Extracts Abstract, methods, results, and claims.
- Council Activation: Activates the council of 5 reviewer agents based on the extracted content.

2. AGENT MODULES (The Council)
- Skeptical Reviewer: Challenges claims, finds weaknesses.
- Method Evaluator: Rigor assessment, validates design.
- Domain Expert: Field knowledge, novelty check.
- Ethics Officer: IRB compliance, bias detection.
- Industry Translator: Practical impact, applicability.

3. DELIBERATION ENGINE
- Scoring Phase: Each reviewer scores the paper on quality, clarity, impact, ethics, and novelty.
- Debate Phase: Agents debate findings, identify limitations, and suggest improvements.
- Report Compilation: The engine generates a detailed feedback report combining all agent inputs.

4. CONSENSUS VERDICT & OUTPUT
- Output Decisions: The system outputs a final verdict of Accept, Minor Revisions, Major Revisions, or Reject.
- Artifacts: The final output includes detailed reasoning, evidence, and recommendations.
